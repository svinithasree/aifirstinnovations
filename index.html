Our Story

Most teams add AI to a product after it is built. The results are usually a chatbot bolted onto an old workflow. We started AI First Innovations on a different question: what would this product look like if AI were part of the design from day one?

That question changes everything, from how data is collected to how people and software share the work. We turn it into working products, pilots and playbooks that organizations can use now.

Today we partner with founders and teams who want to build AI-first, and we run our own experiments to prove what works.
